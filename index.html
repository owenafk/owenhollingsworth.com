More builds

  • Stoplight lamp

    A lamp that runs a traffic-light pattern. A Mega 2560 Pro drives it through a 4-channel relay module, inside a 3D-printed enclosure.

  • PSP-1000 charging stand

    A stand for the original PSP that charges it through pogo pins against the console's factory dock contacts, powered by a USB-C PD trigger board inside the stand. The design is free on MakerWorld.

  • Rack environment monitor

    A printed enclosure that attaches to one of the rack's rear posts and holds an ESP32-C3 Super Mini, an AHT10 temperature and humidity sensor and a rain sensor board, powered over USB-C. It reports to Home Assistant, which notifies me of a leak or high temperatures in my mini-lab. The design is free on MakerWorld.

  • iPad Air display (upcoming)

    My next project: turning a first-generation iPad Air into an external monitor using hardware rather than a screen-sharing app.

  • Off-site backups

    In progress: remote backups of the important data on my mini-lab server, starting with my photos, so a copy lives outside the house.
